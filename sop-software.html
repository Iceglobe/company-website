What S&OP software should do for you

A good S&OP cycle has two jobs: produce a forecast operations can actually deliver, and get every department working from the same picture. Most mid-market manufacturers run it on spreadsheets and ERP exports, which means the monthly review spends its time reconciling numbers instead of making decisions. OmniaOps puts the whole cycle in one connected workspace.

Statistical baseline forecast

Runs several forecasting models on every product and keeps the most accurate one, so planners focus on the products that matter most.

Everyone in the same plan

Sales affiliates and planners adjust forecasts and leave comments in one place. No spreadsheets changing hands.

Supply and inventory in view

Projected inventory, ABC segmentation, safety stock and reorder points from your target service level, with stockout risks flagged early.

S&OP review and dashboards

A consolidated view for the monthly meeting, aligning demand, supply and finance on one page, plus an executive view of the whole business.

Scenario tools

Test best and worst cases for tenders, launches and big customers before you commit to a production plan.

Workflow automation

Planners define the routine, the system runs it, and people make the final call.

Built around your S&OP process, not the other way round

Most planning software asks you to change your process to fit the tool. We start from your cycle: who gives input, when the reviews happen, who signs off. Then we adapt the platform to it, building custom modules and views in days where you need them. Our team has run S&OP inside manufacturers, and implementation includes reviewing your process with you and adjusting it where it makes sense.

New to running S&OP? Our free S&OP training series walks through the cycle step by step, starting with why S&OP matters and building the statistical forecast. You can also score your current process with the S&OP maturity self-assessment.

How implementation works

  1. Get to know your business. A first call about your planning process, where it hurts today and what good would look like. No commitment.
  2. Scope and plan. We map your current process and data flows, agree the future state with you, and give you a rollout timeline with transparent pricing.
  3. Working on your own data in under two weeks. An Excel import gets your team into the platform with real numbers straight away.
  4. Full ERP integration in 4-6 weeks, depending on how many modules you bring online and how mature your data is. Weekly demos keep you in control, and training plus 30 days of post-launch support are included.

Questions buyers ask

What does it cost?

Typical annual subscriptions run from $40,000 to $120,000, depending on sites, SKUs, users and modules. You get an exact quote after the first call.

Which ERPs does it work with?

We have live integrations with SAP Business One and Microsoft Business Central. Any ERP with an API can be connected in 4-6 weeks, through the API, file feeds or SQL replication.

How is this different from Kinaxis, o9 or SAP IBP?

Those platforms are built for very large enterprises and typically take many months to implement with a partner. OmniaOps is built for mid-market manufacturers: weeks to go live, no consulting army, and a platform we adapt to your process. Read our honest comparison of Kinaxis, o9, Anaplan and OmniaOps.

Who is it not for?

If you are a very large enterprise that needs a vendor with decades of references and 24/7 multi-region support today, an established enterprise suite is probably the safer choice.
