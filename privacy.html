Privacy policy

Last updated 9 November 2026

This policy explains what personal data OmniaOps ApS collects when you use omniaops.com, why we collect it, and what rights you have. We keep the data we collect to what we need to answer you and to understand how the site is used.

Who is responsible

The data controller is OmniaOps ApS, CVR 46516834, Greater Copenhagen. Questions about this policy or your data: phlv@omniaops.com.

What we collect and why

WhenDataPurpose and legal basis

Steps taken at your request before a possible contract, and our legitimate interest in replying to business enquiries (GDPR art. 6(1)(b) and (f)).

We do not sell personal data and we do not use it for automated decisions about you.

Services that process data for us

  • GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA) hosts the website.
  • Web3Forms delivers the contents of our forms to our email inbox.
  • Google Analytics 4 (Google Ireland Ltd. / Google LLC) provides site analytics.
  • YouTube (Google) hosts our training videos. Video thumbnails load from YouTube; the player itself only loads, from the privacy-enhanced youtube-nocookie.com domain, when you press play.

Some of these providers process data outside the EU/EEA, including in the USA. Where they do, the transfer relies on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

Cookies

Google Analytics sets cookies (named _ga and _ga_*) that last up to two years. They let us count visits and see how people move through the site. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or install Google's Analytics opt-out add-on. The site works without them.

How long we keep data

  • Enquiries and booking details: up to 24 months after our last contact, unless we enter a customer relationship, in which case the customer agreement applies.
  • Google Analytics data: 14 months.

Your rights

You can ask for access to your data, have it corrected or deleted, restrict or object to our use of it, and receive it in a portable format. Email phlv@omniaops.com and we will reply within one month.

If you think we handle your data incorrectly, you can complain to the Danish Data Protection Agency (Datatilsynet), datatilsynet.dk.

Changes

We update this policy when the way we handle data changes. The date at the top shows the latest version.
