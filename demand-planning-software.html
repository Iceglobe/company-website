Why statistics first

Nobody has time to review thousands of items by hand every month, and most of your revenue comes from a handful of products. Good demand planning software gives you a statistical forecast that is good enough for the long tail, and frees people to focus on the flagship products, launches, tenders and big customers.

Best-fit forecast per product

Several forecasting models run on every product, covering trend and seasonality, autocorrelation, intermittent demand and a moving-average fallback. The best performer is kept for each item and keeps learning from actual sales.

Collaborative input from sales

Sales affiliates and key-account owners add their market knowledge as overrides with comments, so every change has a reason behind it.

Scenarios for big events

Plan tenders with likelihood buckets and launches with best, expected and worst cases before they hit the production plan.

Straight into supply planning

The agreed demand plan flows into projected inventory, safety stock and a purchase plan that updates as forecasts and orders change.

How it fits your planning cycle

Demand planning is the first step of the monthly S&OP cycle: run the statistical baseline, collect input from the sales markets that drive most of your volume and variability, agree the numbers, and hand a plan to operations they can deliver. OmniaOps supports each of those steps in one workspace, with your ERP data kept in sync.

Want the method behind it? Read statistical forecasting for S&OP, which sales affiliates to involve, and how to handle tenders, launches and new customers. To put a number on what missed deliveries cost you today, try the stockout cost calculator.

How implementation works

  1. Get to know your business. A first call about your planning process, where it hurts today and what good would look like. No commitment.
  2. Scope and plan. We map your current process and data flows, agree the future state with you, and give you a rollout timeline with transparent pricing.

Questions buyers ask

What does it cost?

Typical annual subscriptions run from $40,000 to $120,000, depending on sites, SKUs, users and modules. You get an exact quote after the first call.

Which ERPs does it work with?

We have live integrations with SAP Business One and Microsoft Business Central. Any ERP with an API can be connected in 4-6 weeks, through the API, file feeds or SQL replication.

Do we need clean data to start?

No. Start with simple rules to clean your sales history, for example excluding extreme outliers, and refine them as you learn which peaks were real. The Excel import means you can start with the data you have today.

Does it replace our planners?

No. It takes the routine work off their desk. Planners define the rules, the system runs them, and people make the final call on the decisions that matter.
