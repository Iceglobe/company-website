Demand Planning

One workspace that replaces the spreadsheet chaos

Replaces the spreadsheet chaos with a single collaborative workspace. Statistical models do the heavy lifting on forecasts, your team refines them in one place, and the whole process runs faster and with far less back-and-forth.

Statistical Forecasts

Everyone in the Same Plan

Supply Planning

Clear view of inventory risk across your full product range

Takes the legwork out of purchase and production planning. The platform gives you a clear view of projected inventory across your full product range, flags risks before they become stockouts, and generates a ready-to-act purchase plan, updated automatically as forecasts and orders change.

Projected Inventory

Calculates your projected inventory and generates a purchase plan, taking into account customer orders, forecast, placed orders, safety stock, and lead time

ABC Segmentation

Segments your products by ABC and, based on your target service level, calculates your safety stock and re-order point
