Supply Planning Module

Takes the legwork out of purchase and production planning. The platform gives you a clear view of projected inventory across your full product range, flags risks before they become stockouts, and generates a ready-to-act purchase plan - all updated automatically as forecasts and orders change.

  • Calculates your projected inventory and generates a purchase plan, taking into account customer orders, forecast, placed orders, safety stock, and lead time
  • Segments your products by ABC and, based on your target service level, calculates your safety stock and re-order point
